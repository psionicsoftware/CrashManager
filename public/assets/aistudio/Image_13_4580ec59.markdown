## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-precision, tactile, modern sports-hospitality interface tailored for pool, snooker, and billiards hall managers. Balancing high-stress operational speed with the upscale atmosphere of contemporary billiard lounges, the UI prioritizes instantaneous status legibility, effortless turn-around times, and low cognitive overhead.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Clean Minimalism** with **Refined Tactile Accents**:
- Pure, clean surfaces allow active game timers and critical alerts to command visual hierarchy without screen fatigue.
- Skeuomorphic homage is achieved through structural geometry rather than heavy ornament: card corners and pocket accents mirror the refined perimeter of regulation pool tables.
- Monospaced numerical telemetry contrasts against friendly, geometric typography to reinforce operational precision and commercial confidence.

## Colors
The color foundation utilizes a clean slate canvas paired with a high-contrast, status-driven palette that directly informs table states across a busy room.

### Status Semantic System
- **Libre (Available):** Primary Emerald (`#059669`) anchored with subtle emerald-50 tints for vacant tables awaiting players.
- **Ocupada (Active Session):** Electric Cyan/Blue (`#0EA5E9`) signaling ongoing play with ample session balance.
- **Por Vencer (Ending Soon / &lt; 5m):** Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`) creating ambient urgency for staff to prepare closing or billings.
- **Tiempo Cumplido (Time Expired / Overtime):** Urgent Crimson Red (`#EF4444`) generating distinct contrast and pulsing alerts for immediate turnover or per-minute surcharge.
- **Reservada (Booked Ahead):** Deep Violet (`#8B5CF6`) for upcoming scheduled matches and VIP bookings.
- **Mantenimiento (Out of Service / Re-felting):** Neutral Slate (`#64748B`) signaling inactive, non-billable hardware.

### Base Tokens
- **Canvas Neutral:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for reduced eye-strain in dimly lit or artificially illuminated billiard halls.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with micro-borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Text & Content:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for primary copy, `#475569` (Slate 600) for secondary metadata, and `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) for structural iconography.

## Typography
The system employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interface flow, headers, and operational controls due to its modern geometric clarity and open apertures. 

For critical tabular data, table identifiers (e.g., `MESA 008`), and active timers (`01:42:19`), the system uses **JetBrains Mono**. This eliminates horizontal layout shifts caused by ticking digits and facilitates instant peripheral reading from across the counter.

All operational labels and badges use uppercase tracking (`0.04em` to `0.05em`) to clearly separate system-level identifiers from conversational member or order notes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an adaptive fluid-grid framework optimized for POS terminals, counter tablets, and desktop back-offices:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column dynamic grid with `2rem` outer margin and `1.25rem` gutters. Accommodates 3 to 4 table cards per row.
- **Tablet / Counter POS (768px - 1023px):** 6-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins. Table cards snap into a 2-column or 3-column density matrix.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px - 767px):** Single or 2-column view with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters.

Spacing enforces strict structural rhythms: nested card paddings respect `space-md` (16px), dense table stats use `space-sm` (8px), and major operational section dividers scale with `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics tactile, clean surfaces under bright venue task lighting:
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Crisp `#F8FAFC` slate floor, completely matte.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Table Modules):** Pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an ambient drop shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Floating Timers):** Interactive elevations carry a focused, soft-tinted ambient glow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Quick Rate Adjusters):** Floating popovers use a crisp ring outline (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Pocket Accent Motif:** Active table cards feature four corner inner micro-radii or dual-tone pocket indicator dots in deep charcoal/slate to evoke the physical engineering of tournament slate tables without visual clutter.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` (`0.5rem` / 8px standard, `1rem` / 16px for `rounded-lg`, and `1.5rem` / 24px for `rounded-xl`) balances technical rigor with the organic contours of pool tables and phenolic balls.

- **Table Cards:** Standardized to `rounded-lg` (16px) to emulate the cushioned corners of billiard rails.
- **Chips, Pills, and Timers:** Pill-radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for fast visual categorization, quick-touch toggles, and status badges.
- **Inputs & Action Triggers:** Standard `rounded` (8px) for precise alignment and form clarity.

## Components

### 1. Pool Table Cards (Core Operational Unit)
- **Geometry:** White container with `rounded-lg`, surrounded by a 1px `#E2E8F0` border. A 4px vertical status rail on the left or top border instantly transmits table state via color.
- **Corner Pockets:** Subtle rounded corner cutouts or inset 4px slate rivets at the card corners subtlely reflecting billiard pockets.
- **Header:** Table ID (e.g., `MESA 004`) in `label-code` (JetBrains Mono) paired with a status badge (`Libre`, `Ocupada`, etc.) rendered with a soft background tint and a saturated text/dot indicator.
- **Central Counter:** Live running session clock in `label-timer-lg`. For available tables, this displays total idle time or rate configuration (`$12.00/hr`).
- **Footer Controls:** Quick action triggers: "+30m", "Pausar", "Cobrar", or "Abrir Mesa".

### 2. Floating Timer Countdowns & Badges
- **Display:** Pill-shaped badge with high-contrast JetBrains Mono digits.
- **Alert States:**
  - *Normal:* Slate-100 background with Slate-900 digits.
  - *&lt; 5 Min:* Amber-50 background, Amber-700 digits, subtle Amber border pulse.
  - *Overtime:* Crimson-100 background, Crimson-700 digits, persistent warning beacon icon.

### 3. Quick Action Pills & Status Badges
- Encapsulated pills (`rounded-full`) with `space-xs` vertical padding and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Color combinations follow `bg-[color]-50`, `text-[color]-700`, and `border border-[color]-200`.

### 4. Switch View Toggle (Grid / List)
- Segmented dual-state container (`#F1F5F9`) with smooth transition pill indicator.
- Houses SVG icons (Grid vs. Dense Row Table) for managers handling 20+ tables who switch to compact rows during rush hours.

### 5. Rate Adjuster & Tier Selector
- Interactive counter with increment/decrement stepper buttons (`-` / `+`), current multiplier display, and segmented selector for Happy Hour, Member Tier, or Tournament Standard rates.

### 6. Analytics & Revenue Stat Cards
- Minimalist metric cards displaying *Current Shift Gross*, *Active Table Occupancy %*, *Average Turn Time*, and *Pending Invoices*.
- Metric value rendered in `display-lg`, accompanied by a micro sparkline and percentage change pill.

### 7. Form Controls & Selection Inputs
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Emerald-600 active fill with white checks, using `rounded` (4px) for checkboxes and full circles for table selection.
- **Input Fields:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` background, `#E2E8F0` border, transitions to `#059669` ring focus indicator with zero layout shift.
